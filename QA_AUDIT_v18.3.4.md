# Cantoa v18.3.4 QA audit

## Scope
Targeted membership clarity, Create/Advanced parity, language guidance, and preservation of v18.3.3 provider routing.

## Changes
- Creator allowance: 40 new-music minutes/month.
- Studio allowance: 120 new-music minutes/month.
- Stripe webhook grants the same allowances on checkout and renewal.
- Existing-song exports do not consume music-generation minutes.
- Optional Song title and Style are visible in simple Create and remain fully configurable in Advanced.
- My Sound remains available in Advanced.
- Language copy names a broad sample and still permits any typed language/dialect/mix.

## Notes
The music-minute increase is intentionally more generous than v18.3.3 while remaining conservative until real blended provider-cost data is collected across ElevenLabs, Mureka, and Stability.
