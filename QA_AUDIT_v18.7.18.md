# Cantoa v18.7.18 QA audit

Focused metadata/context release.

- Devotional outputs now receive natural editorial metadata instead of raw wording such as “sang by a male.”
- Vocal descriptors adapt to the requested mood (warm/expressive/powerful) rather than always labeling male vocals as powerful.
- Devotional descriptions can include a concise peaceful/prayerful atmosphere cue when supported by the prompt.
- Smart Actions now recognize devotional songs and can suggest “More peaceful” without adding permanent controls.
- No provider routing, fallback, pricing, membership, SEO/indexing, payment, or entitlement logic was changed.
